# Hook & Sell: Fishing to Atlantis

A casual one-finger fishing game built for YouTube Playables.

Drop your hook, dodge the fish on the way down, grab everything on the way up, sell the catch and upgrade your gear, from the Mediterranean coast down to the Kraken in the abyss, then the Bermuda Triangle and the sunken ruins of Atlantis: six zones, six bosses, 31 species.

- Play: https://dvostt.github.io/hook-and-sell/
- Trailer (16:9): https://dvostt.github.io/hook-and-sell/trailer.mp4
- Trailer (9:16): https://dvostt.github.io/hook-and-sell/trailer-vertical.mp4
- Gameplay preview: https://dvostt.github.io/hook-and-sell/preview.mp4

Single HTML5 file, no libraries or external assets, YouTube Playables SDK integrated (a Playgama Bridge build is on Playgama). English, Italian, Russian, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese and German.
